Overview

Brand Identity & Business Website

Defend Health is a healthcare technology company providing integrated solutions that improve patient safety, resident monitoring, and crisis management. I was responsible for designing the company's visual identity and developing its WordPress website from concept through deployment. The project included logo design, business card design, responsive website development, hosting configuration, deployment, and post-launch maintenance.

Project Facts

  • Industry: Healthcare Technology
  • Timeline: Approximately 1 month
  • Role: Logo Designer & WordPress Developer
  • Platform: WordPress
  • Communication: Email
  • Status: Live

Responsibilities

  • Logo Design & Visual Identity
  • Website Design & Responsive Design
  • WordPress Development & Contact Form
  • Hosting Setup & Domain Configuration
  • Deployment & Website Maintenance

Tools Used

  • Design: CorelDRAW, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop
  • Development: WordPress, Elementor
  • Server & Deployment: cPanel, FileZilla

Deliverables

  • Company Logo (Horizontal & Vertical) & Favicon
  • Responsive WordPress Website & Custom CMS
  • Functional Contact Form & Domain/Hosting Setup
